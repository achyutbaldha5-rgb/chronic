Understanding Drip Irrigation

Discover how precision watering maximizes crop yield while saving up to 60% water.

💧

What is Drip Irrigation?

Drip irrigation is a type of micro-irrigation system that slowly and directly delivers water to the base of each plant through a network of pipes, tubes, and emitters. It minimizes water wastage through evaporation, runoff, and deep percolation.

⚙

How Drip Irrigation Works

Water is filtered and supplied through a mainline to the field. It then flows through sub-main and lateral pipes. Emitters attached to the lateral pipes release water drop by drop near the plant roots, ensuring efficient absorption.

Water Source → Pump → Filter → Main Line → Sub Main → Lateral Pipe → Emitter
🎑

Key Benefits of Drip Irrigation

  • ✓ Up to 60% Water Savings compared to flood irrigation
  • ✓ Higher Crop Yield & superior produce quality
  • ✓ Fertigation Ready (apply nutrients directly to roots)
  • ✓ Reduces Weed Growth & prevents soil erosion
  • ✓ Better Soil Health (maintains soil structure & aeration)
  • ✓ Saves Time, Labor & Energy with uniform distribution
