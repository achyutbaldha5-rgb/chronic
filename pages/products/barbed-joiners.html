Barbed Drip Line Joiners (16mm)

Leak-proof 16mm barbed fittings with high pull-out resistance

Chronic Industries 16mm Barbed Drip Line Joiners are precision-molded from high-grade polypropylene for a watertight, secure connection every time. The multi-barb design grips the lateral pipe firmly to prevent pull-out even under high pressure.

Available in straight joiners, elbows, tees, and end-caps — these fittings are a complete lateral pipe connection solution. They require no tools or adhesives for installation, making field assembly fast and reliable.
