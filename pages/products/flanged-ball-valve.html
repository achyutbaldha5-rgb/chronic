Flanged Poly Ball Valve

Full-bore quarter-turn shutoff with zero leak — UV and chemical proof

The Chronic Industries Flanged Poly Ball Valve is a full-bore polypropylene valve with precision-machined PTFE seats for a bubble-tight shutoff. Its quarter-turn operation allows instant open/close flow control with minimal effort.

Manufactured from UV and chemical-resistant polypropylene, it performs reliably in outdoor irrigation environments, fertilizer injection systems, and chemical dosing applications.
