Heavy-Duty Clamp Filter Body

Quick-release SS clamp body for high-pressure main farm line filtration

The Chronic Industries Heavy-Duty Clamp Filter Body is a premium polypropylene filter housing secured with a stainless steel quick-release clamp. It is engineered for the demanding conditions of main farm line filtration, where pressure and flow volumes are high.

The stainless steel clamp enables tool-free maintenance in under 30 seconds — simply open the clamp, remove the cartridge, rinse, and re-insert. Compatible with disc and screen filter elements.

Technical Specifications
